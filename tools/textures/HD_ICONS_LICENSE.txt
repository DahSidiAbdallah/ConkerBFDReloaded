HD Icons for Conker's Bad Fur Day: Reloaded
===========================================

Pack by dahmedvall95.

Textures by GameBeast92, from the Conker's Bad Fur Day 4K Ultimate Texture Pack:
https://github.com/GameBeast92/Conker-s-Bad-Fur-Day-4k-Ultimate-Texture-Pack
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This pack is a modified version of those textures, and is shared under the same
license (CC BY 4.0). Changes made:
- Only the 2D pictures are used (HUD, menus, on-screen text and logos).
- Colours the original pack changed are put back to the original game's colours
  (e.g. the buttons' green lettering, the PAUSED title's purple and gold).
- Shapes added that the original game doesn't have are removed (e.g. dots after QUIT).
- Pictures the game draws in several pieces are joined before processing, so the
  pieces line up without seams.
- The glowing (selected) button pictures are remade from the sharp normal ones.
- The Dolby and Nintendo logos are the original pictures redrawn with clean edges.
- The save menu's NEW GAME button keeps the pack's frame with its lettering redrawn
  (the pack has no HD version of it).
- Pictures are resized to at most 8 times the original size.
