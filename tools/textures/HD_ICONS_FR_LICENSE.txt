HD Icons (French) for Conker's Bad Fur Day: Reloaded
====================================================

The HD Icons pack with the menus, titles and logos of the French translation, for
playing the game translated into French (see the README: you patch your own ROM).

French translation by Corrigo (text) and Djipi (graphics), from emulation64.fr.
The French graphics are Djipi's, used with his permission (and Corrigo's).
